# Week of 15 to 21 November 2026

Everything below is live on the server.

## Bounties

- `/bounty <player> <amount>` puts money on someone's head, at least 100. The money is taken at once
- Bounties on the same player add up, and whoever kills them gets the whole pot with a server announcement
- `/bounty` on its own shows the biggest bounties, highest first, and `/bounty <player>` shows what is on one player

## Friends, parties and clans

- `/friend add <player>` sends a friend request, and they have to accept. Friends get a message when you join or leave, and `/friend` opens a menu of your friends with online ones first. Up to 100 friends each
- `/party invite <player>` starts a party of up to 8, with its own chat (`/party chat`). Party members can't hurt each other
- `/clan create <name> <tag>` makes a clan of up to 20 members, free to make. The clan's `[TAG]` shows before every member's name in chat, and the clan has its own chat (`/clan chat`)
- Names like admin, staff, mod or owner can't be taken as a clan name or tag

## Crates and cosmetics

- `/crates` sells keys for 5,000 in-game money each, 1 or 5 at a time. Each key opens one cosmetic after a short spin: a chat tag or a walking trail
- 8 tags and 8 trails in three tiers: common, rare and legendary. A new legendary is announced to the server
- A duplicate gives back a quarter of what the key cost
- `/cosmetics` lets you wear a tag before your name in chat, or turn a trail on and off
- Cosmetics only, no gear, money or XP, and the money spent on keys leaves the economy
