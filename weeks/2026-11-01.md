# Week of 1 to 7 November 2026

Everything below is live on the server.

## Hyzen Coins

- A second currency, earned just by playing: 1 coin every 10 minutes AFK, every 8 moving around, every 6 mining or building, every 5 fighting
- AFK machines, minecarts, water streams and autoclickers only earn the AFK rate, and only one account per IP earns
- `/hc` shows your coins, your current rate and time to the next coin; `/hc top` shows the most coins
- `/hcshop` to spend them: netherite ingots, upgrade templates, elytra (1 a week), enchanted golden apples (1 a day), Mending books, shulker shells and XP bottles, with a confirm screen so nobody buys by accident
- Admins get `/hc give|take|set|check`. Rates, items, prices and limits are all in the config

## Chat

- Swear filter on chat, private messages, signs, item names and AH listings. Normal chat with prices and numbers still goes through
- Three warnings, then an automatic 1-day mute; warnings clear after 7 days. Mods get `/mute` and `/unmute`, helpers get `/warnings <player>`
- The [+] and [-] join and leave lines are gone
