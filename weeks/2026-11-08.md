# Week of 8 to 14 November 2026

Everything below is live on the server.

## Buy orders

- `/orders` lets you post what you want to buy, how many, and the price per item. The money is held as soon as you post, so every order can pay
- Anyone can fill an order from their inventory, all of it or part of it, and is paid on the spot. The items go to the buyer's mailbox
- Up to 5 open orders per player. Cancel any time, or wait 7 days, and the unfilled part is refunded
- Worn tools, and items that don't match exactly (wrong enchantments, wrong potion), are refused, so nobody gets scammed

## Protection and voice chat

- `hyzensmp.net` now runs through DDoS protection, so attacks on the server address are absorbed before they reach it
- The server sees each player's real connection again behind that protection, so the one-account-per-IP rule for Hyzen Coins works as intended
- Voice chat works with the protected address: players keep using the Simple Voice Chat mod as before, nothing to change on their side
