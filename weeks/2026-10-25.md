# Week of 25 to 31 October 2026

Everything below is live on the server.

## Staff tools

- `/ban <player> <reason> <time>` with timed bans (30m, 7d...) or permanent blacklists, plus `/unban`, `/banhistory`, and `/banrollback` to undo every ban one staff member made
- `/vanish`, `/staffmode` (vanish + flight + no damage) and `/sc` staff chat. Vanished staff are hidden from tab, chat, the server list and leaderboards, and can open chests silently
- `/sus`: a menu of every player the anti-cheat has flagged, with one-click ban buttons
- Auto-totem detection: staff get an alert when someone re-equips a totem faster than a human can
- `/totempop <player>` to test a suspect's re-totem speed (it can't kill them and gives the totem back)
- `/perf` shows server performance, and staff get an alert if the server starts lagging

## New players

- A short guide in chat when they first land, with clickable commands
- A starter kit (stone tools and food, no gear) and three starter goals with small rewards
- A one-time bonus for their first sales in their first 2 hours, so the first hour feels rewarding

## Rewards and competition

- `/daily` reward with a login streak, plus playtime rewards (items only, no gear, so it stays fair)
- `/leaderboard` for money, kills, mob kills and playtime, resetting daily, weekly and monthly

## Selling

- `/sell` now opens a sell window: drop items in, see the total, confirm. `/sell hand` still sells what you hold
- `/worth` to check prices, `/sellhistory` to see past sales

## Server protection

- Ender pearl cooldown is now 10s (wind charge 5s, chorus fruit 2s)
- Bedrock, barriers and command blocks can't be placed or used
- Limits on minecarts, boats and armour stands per chunk, and automatic clearing of item piles that cause lag
- The known dupe methods are switched off, and every update checks they stay off
- Hoppers are a bit faster

## Also live

- `/shop` is now a DonutSMP-style hub (End, Nether, Gear, Food, Materials)
- Players can move or hide their scoreboard in `/settings`
- Anti-cheat (GrimAC) is back in alerts-only mode

## Testing

Everything was tested with automated bot players before going live: 71 test scenarios covering every feature, all passing.
